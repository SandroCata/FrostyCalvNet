Predict the lines of glaciers in satellitar images and use the lines detected to compare the melting evolution through years.

1) Through Segmentation and Deep Learning, relying on solid neural networks like probabilistic or bayesian U-Net on SAT images or DeepLab and Mask R-CNN. 
This could help also to get an estimation on the uncertainty of predictions.

2) An idea is to combine CNN and Transformer model to improve generalization and precision (see "Towards Global Glacier Mapping with Deep Learning and Open Earth Observation Data" study) 

3) Pre and Post elaboration of images to deal with different quality of images.

4) Integrate with auxiliary data like topographical, climate and in-site measurement to give a correlation to the glacier's changings.
